About Me

I like building things that move, blink, and occasionally write. I document my experiments so others can remix them. When I’m not iterating on a robotic arm or polishing a script, I slow down with calligraphy to remember that good lines take time.

Interests: robotics, embedded systems, programming, UI polish, and the drama of black ink on white paper.

Now

  • Prototyping a humanoid arm (acrylic → metal, torque budgeting, control).
  • Writing posts about process and pitfalls.
  • Practicing copperplate & modern script on weekends.

Based in India • Open to collaborations

Latest Post — Embracing Creativity and Technology

Life is a balance between art and technology. I find joy in coding complex systems as much as I do in expressing myself through calligraphy and design. Technology gives us tools to shape the future, while art connects us to the present. This blog is my corner where I share thoughts, creations, and experiments — both technical and artistic.

Whether it’s experimenting with robotics (like my humanoid arm build), crafting intuitive UIs, or showcasing the elegance of handwritten calligraphy, my goal is to inspire others to merge creativity with logic. The best innovations are born when art meets science.

“Your comfort zone may feel safe, but growth often lives on the other side.”

I carry not just the skills I’ve learned, but the mindset to keep pushing boundaries. Challenges aren’t roadblocks — they’re launchpads for the next leap.
